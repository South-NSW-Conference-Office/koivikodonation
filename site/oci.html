Giving from the USA

To get a US tax receipt, give through OCI. OCI is a US non-profit that can pass your gift on to Koivikko and send you a tax-deductible receipt.

  1. Continue to OCI’s donation pageIt opens in a new tab, so you can come back here anytime.
  2. Click “Select a ministry” and search “Koivikko”Choose Koivikko Lifestyle Institute so your gift reaches Koivikko.
  3. Choose your amount and how oftenSingle payment, monthly or yearly. Card, PayPal, Zelle or check are all accepted.
  4. Get your US tax receipt from OCIOCI is a 501(c)(3) non-profit (EIN 58-1570279) and emails your tax-deductible receipt.

OCI keeps 3% of each gift to support its regional work and accountability, as stated on OCI’s donation page.
